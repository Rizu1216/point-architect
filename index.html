Point Architects is based in Kalaburagi, Karnataka, with work spanning residential villas, apartments, commercial buildings, hospitals, interiors, renovations and turnkey construction projects.

Our design approach balances contemporary design with functionality, local understanding, quality construction and attention to detail.

We work closely with clients, engineers, contractors, vendors and site teams, keeping budgeting, materials, workmanship and project management connected to the design.

Context. Function. Detail. Three principles guide every space we shape.
