The Studio

Thoughtful architecture for the way people live, work and connect.

Point Architects is an architecture and design studio led by Ar. Shaikh Tousif Husen and Syed Mohammad Ayyub Zeeshan. Our portfolio brings together residential, healthcare and other built-environment projects with a focus on practical planning and distinctive visual character.

People Behind Point

Ar. Shaikh Tousif Husen

CO-FOUNDER • PRINCIPAL ARCHITECT

Ar. Shaikh Tousif Husen is an architect and entrepreneur based in Kalaburagi, Karnataka, with a growing presence in architecture, interior design, and construction. As the founder of Point Architects, he believes that good architecture is not only about creating beautiful spaces, but also about creating places that are practical, durable, meaningful, and built to last. Through Point Architects, Tousif has been involved in residential villas, apartments, commercial buildings, hospitals, interiors, renovations, and turnkey construction projects. His approach combines contemporary design with functionality, local understanding, quality construction, and close attention to detail. With hands-on involvement beyond design, he works closely with clients, engineers, contractors, vendors, and site teams throughout construction, giving him practical experience in execution, budgeting, materials, workmanship, and project management. His work reflects a preference for modern, elegant, and timeless design with a strong focus on functionality and client requirements. He also has experience in projects requiring detailed coordination and technical planning, including healthcare and large-scale residential developments. Beyond architecture, Tousif aims to build Point Architects into a respected regional design-and-construction organization known for professional service, quality construction, innovative design, and transparent client relationships. For Tousif, architecture is both a profession and a responsibility — an opportunity to transform ideas into spaces where people live, work, heal, and build their futures. Founder — Point Architects Architecture | Interior | Construction Kalaburagi, Karnataka

Syed Mohammad Ayyub Zeeshan

CO-FOUNDER • PRINCIPAL ARCHITECT

Ar. Syed Mohammad Ayyub Zeeshan is a Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch) graduate and an integral part of the professional journey behind Point Architects. Serving as Co-Founder and Project Architect, he has been a trusted professional partner since 2017. With nearly a decade of collaboration, Zeeshan has played an important role in translating architectural concepts into successfully executed projects. His involvement spans project planning, design coordination, site execution, client interaction, and ensuring that architectural ideas are carried through with attention to quality and detail. One of his strongest professional qualities is his excellent communication and coordination skills. His ability to communicate effectively with clients, consultants, contractors, engineers, and site teams has contributed significantly to smooth project execution and professional relationships. Since 2017, his partnership with Ar. Shaikh Tousif Husen has grown from a professional association into a strong working relationship built on trust, communication, shared responsibility, and a common vision for architecture and construction. As Co-Founder and Project Architect, Zeeshan continues to contribute to the growth of Point Architects with a practical, collaborative, and project-focused approach.
