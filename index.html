01 / ABOUT US

Built around
good ideas.

Architecture should not only look good. It should work well, respond to its surroundings and remain practical throughout its life.

Point Architects is an architecture, interior design and construction practice founded by Ar. Shaikh Tousif Husen.

Based in Kalaburagi, Karnataka, the practice works across residential, commercial, healthcare, interior, renovation and turnkey construction projects.

Our approach balances contemporary design, functionality, local understanding, quality construction and attention to detail. We remain practically involved with clients, engineers, contractors, vendors and site teams throughout the project.
